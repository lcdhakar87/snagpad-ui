ABOUT US

We believe that most job seekers have been trained how to do a job but not how to conduct a job search. Further, professionals who support people during the job search cannot be with them all the time. MPloy represents a link between job seeker and professional in one simple to use online platform. MPloy is not about providing job leads but rather a place where both job seekers and professionals can conduct a strategic job search together. We're all about building a better job seeker.
